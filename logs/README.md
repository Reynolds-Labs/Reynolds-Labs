## About The Logs

Welcome to The Logs, a collection of technical essays, personal perspectives, software evaluations, field notes, and occasional adventures into the stranger corners of technology.

While the Reynolds Labs technical projects focus on building, configuring, and testing systems, The Logs explore the ideas, experiences, and observations behind the technology.

Some entries are serious technical discussions. Others are personal reflections, first impressions, or simply an opportunity to have a little fun with technology.

Every entry receives a permanent log number and publication date. Articles are organized by subject rather than chronological order, allowing readers to explore whichever topics interest them.

## Log Identification & Organization

Each published article follows a consistent identification system.

**Example:**

`LOG-001 — Linux: More Than an Operating System`

### Numbering

- Every new article receives the next available log number.
- Log numbers represent publication order, not subject or category.
- Once assigned, a log number never changes.
- Articles may belong to multiple categories.
- Categories can be reorganized without changing log identifiers.
- Published articles may be updated while retaining their original publication date.

### Article Format

Each article includes:

- A permanent log identifier.
- An article title.
- An original publication date.
- One or more categories.
- The article content.

### Repository Organization

Each article is stored in its own directory.

```text
logs/
├── README.md
├── log001-linux-more-than-an-operating-system/
│   └── README.md
├── log002-future-article/
│   └── README.md
└── ...
```

Future article directories are created as entries are published.
